Kerotakis — privacy policy Last updated 25 August 2026 · applies to the web app, the macOS app and the iOS app

Kerotakis collects nothing, transmits nothing, and has no servers. The chemistry is computed on your own device. You can put the device in aeroplane mode and lose no functionality at all.

What is collected

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. No identifier is generated, read or shared. Nothing about you or your device leaves it.

Where your work is kept

  • Your bench — the vessels, the command history, the register you chose — lives in the running app's memory, and in your browser's local storage on the web.
  • Files you save (.lab scripts, notebooks, charts) go where you tell the system save panel to put them, and nowhere else.
  • Files you open are read into the app and not copied anywhere.
  • None of it is ever uploaded. Clearing site data, or deleting the app, removes it.

Network use

The macOS and iOS apps make no network requests: the solver, the thermodynamic databases and every lesson are compiled into the app, and the macOS build ships without any network entitlement at all, so the operating system would refuse one.

The web version downloads the app and its engine from the site that serves it, then caches them so later visits work offline. Those are ordinary requests for the page's own files; whoever hosts the site (currently GitHub Pages) may keep standard web-server logs, which is outside our control and the only reason this section is not also empty.

Children

Kerotakis is suitable for classroom use. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Third parties

There are none. No SDKs, no ad networks, no analytics vendors. The app bundles open-source components — chiefly IPhreeqc from the U.S. Geological Survey — but these are libraries linked into the binary, not services it talks to.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version appears at this address with a new date above. Since the honest version of this document is "nothing happens", a change would mean the app started doing something it does not do today, and that would be said plainly here.
