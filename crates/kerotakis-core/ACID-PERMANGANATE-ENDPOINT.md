# Bounded acidic permanganate/peroxide acceptance contract

Frozen before the first candidate execution, 2026-10-16. This is a stoichiometric
engineering endpoint with no adopted rate, heat or empirical calibration.

The independently balanced equation is 2 MnO4- + 5 H2O2 + 6 H+ -> 2 Mn+2 +
5 O2 + 8 H2O. Stoichiometry references: UCSB Science Line key164
(https://scienceline.ucsb.edu/getkey.php?key=164), and Alonso de la Peña et al.,
Dental Materials Journal32 (2013), methods p655
(https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/dmj/32/4/32_2012-317/_pdf).
The factual equation is independently authored curated data under CC BY4.0;
no source prose/table was imported. These sources do not establish the chosen
implementation-domain pH ceiling3 or a general rate/heat model.

Require a trusted current complete aqueous answer, positive liquid water,
finite eligible dissolved pools, complete sulfate/potassium counterions and
actually owned aqueous H+ coordinates. Clamp extent by MnO4/2, peroxide/5,
owned H+/6 and existing finite free/unspent acidity/6. Require extent>1e-12mol.
Exclude chloride and other competing mixtures. Solids/gases cannot be consumed
as dissolved reactants. After fresh recharacterization, if BOTH eligible
reactants remain above one trace extent but pH exceeds3, roll back the complete
candidate and report the unsupported acid-depleted competing-product domain.
A fully exhausted reactant does not impose a blanket final pH on Mn(II) products.
Physical KMnO4 stock reaches the ionic route after
normal speciation; no inaccessible nominal molecular sibling is installed.

Core controls must establish each limiting reagent, exact per-extent H/O/Mn/K/S
and charge closure including external O2, preservation of excess/trace material,
wrong-phase and proxy-only acidity rejection, dry/neutral/basic/uncharacterized
and wrong-medium decline, no pre-aqueous application, and full rollback on
failed recharacterization. Open O2 uses GasEvolved; fixed sealed O2 uses
GasContained and retained inventory. Preserve initial oxygen separately.

Real native controls must use the standard wrapper, both stock-addition orders,
open/sealed boundaries, final computed readback, and exported cache replay plus
missing current-question rollback. Real Wasm tests must exercise the shared
wrapper with actual hook/cache answers; mechanical continuation callbacks alone
are insufficient. Preserve thermal ownership through phase-tail re-entry and
reset it at the next operator. Existing background peroxide kinetics remain a
separate source of oxygen on waits.

Acceptance is pending until these targets and applicable native/Wasm/browser
and full own-fleet jobs execute on the exact candidate source. Keep original
