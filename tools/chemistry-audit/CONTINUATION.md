# Chemistry audit continuation

The next scientific gap is time-dependent acid–metal chemistry. Implement it in
the order ELEC-001, ELEC-003, ELEC-004, ELEC-006 and ELEC-008: explicit electrode
surface state; licensed, reviewed parameter records with validity ranges;
Butler–Volmer/Tafel limits; transport limits; then deposit/passivation state.
Acceptance needs matched controls over material, exposed area or particle size,
temperature, acid activity and elapsed time. Do not infer elapsed reaction from
the existing qualitative overpotential table or introduce a universal rate.

Private source research and exact source mappings remain outside the public
repository. Do not revive PR #504.
