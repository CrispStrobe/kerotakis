Fifty new experiments were designed independently by three authors, with physical forecasts frozen before any CLI execution. They use gas laws and spot tests, conductivity, calorimetry, electrochemistry, photochemistry, separations, spectroscopy, redox reactions, and titration. Only command syntax/help informed the initial scripts; implementation and registry inspection came after the freeze.

The original replay made 100 actual CLI invocations, one text and one JSON run per experiment. Fifteen scripts refused in both modes: thirteen on ingredient identities and two on zero-valued controls. The evidence contains all successes and refusals, with executable, forecast, and output hashes. A successful workflow means the evidence was collected, not that the chemistry passed.

The original assessment is **28 agreements, 4 physical failures, 10 capability gaps, and 8 naming/fixture gaps**. The four physical failures share two causes: the electrolysis charge allocation affects experiments 074, 075 and 077; the sealed freeze–thaw failure affects 097. Ten separately frozen naming/alternative-fixture followups all executed successfully. Replacing argon with helium is an alternate fixture, not evidence that argon is supported. Piston volumes also revealed an omitted forecast assumption: regulation adds room-air inventory, so the volume includes the initial 100 mL of background gas.

The source repairs address demonstrated general problems:

- **Electrolysis electron and atom budgets.** The primary hydrogen cathode had been counted as though its charge went to plated metal. Sulfate/water electrolysis therefore left a full electron-equivalent dose of spurious acid; chloride electrolysis omitted cathodic alkali and its water debit. The repaired allocation distinguishes primary hydrogen, metal deposition, and hydrogen overflow. Independent tests close electrons, atoms and charge for both anodes and no/full/partial metal plating. The CLI also declares inert-electrode and selectivity assumptions rather than claiming a measured universal chlorine/oxygen yield.
- **Complete distillation requests.** A missing intermediate Antoine/cascade answer could return a successful partial cut. Experiment 098 requested 0.1 mol per receiver but obtained about 0.08828 and 0.05977 mol for one and two stages. The VLE function now refuses the complete request when an intermediate calculation is unavailable. The Bench preserves all physical state and does not invoke unrelated settling after refusal. Supported small cuts remain positive controls; the fitted range is unchanged.
- **Chromatographic sample coverage.** Correcting the acetic-acid ingredient in 082 revealed that a mapped ethanol peak hid acetic acid and ethyl acetate with missing retention data. The shared sample calculation now reports mapped peaks, ions outside the method, and unparameterised neutrals separately. The typed event and all narration levels disclose incomplete coverage; unknown elution is not described as water-front elution. Relative peak areas are explicitly relative to the largest modeled peak. Saved events remain readable through a defaulted additive field.
- **Unaccepted thermal proposals.** Experiment 097 cooled sealed water, then returned the same heat. PHREEQC failed on the frozen trace-liquid state; the app published ice at 226 °C. Heating now atomically refuses a proposal that leaves observable ice above the melting boundary when the phase route fails or gives no answer. Tests cover complete rollback over several scales and preserve valid sensible heating and successful melting. This closes the false hot-ice result; it does **not** establish an accurate sealed freeze–thaw calculation.
- **Pressure-boundary visibility.** Default piston narration now includes the amount of added boundary gas already present in the typed event. The original pressure-law forecasts and their air-inventory assumption remain unchanged.

Remaining scientific scope is explicit. The sealed aqueous/headspace/ice loop still needs a consistent energy account at residual-brine activity boundaries and robust speciation during thawing. The batch also exposes missing argon and silver-bromide bench identities, absent photolysis coupling, iodide/iodometry coverage, buffered permanganate solid-phase eligibility, and incomplete optical/retention data. These require reviewed identities, thermodynamics, phase ownership or rate/optical evidence; none was replaced with invented constants. An explicit supported-domain refusal can be correct behavior while still leaving an experiment unsupported.

Three further controls were frozen before their post-repair replay: canonical propanone for acetone, pure-water freezing without trapped air, and a supported 1% distillation cut. They isolate naming, coupling and fitted-range effects without modifying the original fifty forecasts.

Resource handling was part of execution. Agents did light design/source work only when load and available memory allowed. Local CLI execution was stopped before its first experiment because load exceeded the gate. All experiment replays and native/WASM validation were moved to isolated hosted runners. Large executable/test artifacts and temporary downloads stayed on `/mnt/storage`; local disk later fell below 1 GiB and `/tmp` briefly filled. No local compilation was started and no dirty or unmerged worktrees were removed.

The first three validation attempts are retained: they caught private helper use, ambiguous type inference, and a missing test import. These integration mistakes were corrected before final validation. Final validation and post-repair evidence are recorded below once complete.

The remaining gaps need systematic acceptance criteria, not one-off expected-output adjustments:

| Gap exposed by this batch | Required closure and independent acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Sealed freezing/thawing (097) | Account for accepted and unaccepted energy at every activity-domain boundary; resume speciation from a valid liquid state. Test cooling/heating reciprocity across sample sizes, air inventories, electrolyte concentrations, partial and complete freezing, and solver refusal. Conservation and phase temperature bounds must hold even when convergence fails. |
| Distillation domain (098) | Extend bubble-point/cascade support only with reviewed vapour-pressure data; sweep composition, pressure, cut size and stage count. Each successful fraction request must complete its mass budget; every domain exit must leave the complete Bench unchanged. |
| Chromatography and optics (082, 088, 099) | Keep sample-wide coverage separate from the supported response. Missing neutral retention or complex optical bands must remain visible alongside supported peaks/bands; validate parameters against independently sourced measurements before expanding coverage. |
| Identity and input handling (052–058, 064–066, 079, 083–084, 093) | Separate aliases for an existing substance from genuinely missing species. Preserve canonical stored identities, give actionable input diagnostics, and test alias equivalence without silently substituting a different chemical. |
| Photochemistry (080–081) | Couple an explicitly supported absorption/quantum-yield mechanism to photon dose; validate wavelength, irradiance, dark and zero-input controls. Equal uncoupled red/UV/dark output is a capability gap, not evidence of photochemical equivalence. |
| Redox and solid eligibility (085, 095) | Review dataset components, oxidation-state ownership and admissible phases together. Validate both products and electron/atom budgets; do not treat an unoffered supersaturated mineral or persistent indicator colour as a successful reaction. |
