# Titration quantities and finite-stock accounting

This source-informed follow-up closes the dose/stock gaps recorded by the titration safety audit. Thirty-two contracts were frozen at `de3a8b51` before behavioral baseline execution. Baseline [37197016380](https://github.com/CrispStrobe/kerotakis/actions/runs/37197016380) confirmed **26 failures and six supported controls**. Ten supplementary inspection/review controls were frozen in five later commits before their repair execution; those ten have no separate pre-repair baseline. All forty-two frozen test hashes are retained unchanged.

## Baseline findings

Titration bypassed shelf limits for both titrant and carrier water. Receiver increments could be swallowed, rounded inaccurately or overflow. Nonfinite inputs, two negative operands and invalid solver proposals were not rejected appropriately. Subnormal endpoint fractions could round to zero or incur large relative errors, while a provisional full dose could still be committed. Early solver failure could be described as exhaustion of the configured colour/potential step limit.

The general stock ledger used an absolute minimum exhaustion allowance of `1e-9` in every unit. An empty bottle therefore allowed arbitrarily many sufficiently small positive draws. Tiny draws against huge stock could succeed without a debit. Negative or nonfinite requests could increase or erase stock. These ledger findings affect ordinary species/material dispensing as well as titration.

## Repair

Titration validates concentration and volume separately, validates the active endpoint and nominal titrant/carrier conversions, and rejects nonfinite initial pH/pe chart points before trials. Product checks divide by the smaller operand first: either fixed division order can mask subnormal rounding by quantizing its intermediate quotient. Exact supported subnormal products remain available.

Every full/fractional proposal requires positive, finite, accurately scaled titrant/carrier/volume quantities. Checked molecular receiver deposits enforce actual increment accuracy within relative `1e-8`, including combined aqueous/liquid inventory. The raw proposal must satisfy the normal state certificate before screening or solving, which catches aggregate mass/energy overflow even when individual coordinates are finite. A successful solver result must also satisfy the certificate before it can become an accepted titration proposal. Numeric fractional failures stop the current increment rather than falling back to a provisional full dose. Cumulative delivered volume and the chart's millilitre conversion are checked before commit.

Virtual endpoint trials do not consume shelf stock. After selecting the accepted fraction, titrant and carrier requests are reserved on a cloned ledger; only when every draw succeeds are the flask and ledger assigned together. Water titrant combines both roles into one canonical water request and checks that neither contribution disappears in the combined amount. A virtual full dose may exceed available stock when its final refined fraction fits. Exhaustion or unrepresentable stock debit discards the current proposal and warnings while retaining prior accepted progress. Progress is counted from committed doses rather than pH-curve length, so a source with no initial pH still reports its first characterized accepted dose correctly. The final pH comes from the current accepted solution, rather than the last historical curve point. An accepted raw dose with no fresh characterization does not invent a titration summary from stale initial pH.

The stock ledger rejects negative/nonfinite requests even for untracked keys. It removes the absolute allowance for empty or microscopic bottles, and requires the represented debit to agree with the requested positive withdrawal within relative `1e-8`. Final-bit exhaustion remains supported using a relative allowance; this is a quantified floating-point tolerance, not exact arbitrary-precision conservation. Zero draws and absent-key unlimited supplies retain their behavior. New `StockRefusal::InvalidRequest` and `StockRefusal::Precision` distinguish invalid/unrepresentable requests from real shortage. External exhaustive Rust matches on that public enum need to handle the added variants.

Ordinary species/material stock refusals now skip settlement. Invalid ordinary `add` amounts retain their existing hard-error/no-journal contract. New quantity and stock-precision diagnostics have German and French messages. Early-stop diagnostics no longer claim the configured step limit was exhausted after a veto, stock/numeric refusal, missing characterization or full-dose solver failure.

Initial load was about 1.3, available memory about 6 GiB and both local disks had about 5.3 GiB free. Two read-only agents reviewed stock semantics and numerical precision independently; neither edited files or launched builds. The numerical review caught the symmetric operand-order masking and invalid initial-chart-point gaps. This repair establishes per-dose refusal with prior accepted progress retained, not a complete-command rollback after later refusal. Shelf stock measures canonical species in moles and material recipes in their existing basis units; it does not introduce a separate prepared-burette inventory or solvent displacement model. The spill-recovery probe and quantity matrix was subsequently addressed by the [recovery follow-up](../spill-recovery-contracts-20261004/RESULTS.md). The extraction probe/quantity matrix and stable partition calculations were subsequently addressed by the [extraction audit](../extraction-contracts-20261004/RESULTS.md). Post-equilibration safety, per-stage extraction thermal/geometry policy, still-cut donor debit accuracy near an ULP, and persisted compensated quantity representation remain further work. The state certificate establishes coordinate validity; it does not by itself prove every custom solver's chemical element conservation.

## Follow-up contract targets

The [spill recovery audit](../spill-recovery-contracts-20261004/RESULTS.md) subsequently froze and validated the recovery matrix. The [extraction audit](../extraction-contracts-20261004/RESULTS.md) subsequently froze and validated extraction controls around the same three observable properties: the screening proposal matches the actual accepted quantities, a veto prevents settlement and mutation, and tiny donor/receiver changes cannot disappear silently. Still-cut donor tests should compare represented debit with requested debit near an ULP while retaining the existing supported tiny-cut controls. Broader numerical work needs a deliberate persisted quantity representation rather than tightening tolerances until ordinary calculations fail. Post-equilibration safety and custom-solver conservation require separate policy contracts because coordinate validity alone cannot establish either.
