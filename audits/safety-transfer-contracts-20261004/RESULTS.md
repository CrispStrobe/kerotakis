## Repair

At the ordinary command boundary, any `SafetyVeto` restores the complete physical bench checkpoint, drops speculative physical-change events, journals the veto and skips all subsequent heat propagation and solver settlement. This covers stock, vessels, spills and broken-vessel state. Accidental spills still emit hazard diagnostics and happen; they do not become refusable commands.

Decant, mix and filter prepare a receiver containing the incoming molecular and unresolved liquid inventory, the disturbed surface geometry, and the existing adiabatic energy balance. The safety screen assesses this prepared receiver; an allowed operation applies its inventory and temperature directly. This avoids a separate safety-only reconstruction diverging from the actual transfer. Surface event reporting and prepared donor precision checks remain in place. Filtration transfers declared unresolved liquid portions while retaining solids, headspace gas and nonliquid unresolved material.

The energy balance continues to price molecular portions using the existing heat-capacity data. This change does not add heat-capacity data for unresolved recipes, nor does it promise safety screening after chemical equilibration. The screen sees the raw operation proposal before solver settlement.
