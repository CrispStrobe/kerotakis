## Scientific changes

Ethanol/water calculations previously reconstructed water as `1 - ethanol_fraction`. A positive water amount disappeared whenever the ethanol scalar rounded to one. The bubble-point API now accepts independent ethanol and water mole amounts, and the binary still carries both coordinates through UNIFAC and every stage. The existing scalar API remains compatible; callers with a separately represented trace can use `ethanol_water_bubble_point_from_moles`. Dew and TP activity callbacks also retain both coordinates returned by the generic solver. Actual normalization underflow remains an explicit refusal.

The still accumulates condensate and derives residue from the original inventory minus that condensate. Positive trace controls cover both orientations at 1e-14, 1e-20 and 1e-100. Independent infinite-dilution Rayleigh expectations use `trace * -expm1(alpha^stages * ln1p(-fraction))`, where alpha is the trace activity coefficient times its saturation pressure divided by total pressure at the pure solvent bubble point. The first-order 1024-step integration must agree within 1e-3 relative to trace condensate; separate checks cover the cut, latent heat and final bubble point. These are numerical model checks, not empirical validation of UNIFAC.

Both binary and general stills now preflight receiver additions before withdrawing material. A positive cut must remain finite and its represented increment must agree with the requested amount within 1e-8 relative error. The check covers both the target liquid portion and aggregate liquid/aqueous inventory that settlement may merge. Failure leaves vessels, temperatures and solver cache unchanged, performs no settlement, and explains that an empty receiver can retain this trace cut. Existing frozen preloaded-receiver refusals remain valid while new empty-receiver controls require positive trace transfer.
