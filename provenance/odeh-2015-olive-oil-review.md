# Olive-oil geometry datum review

Odeh, Musameh and Abdelraziq (2015), *Influence of Waste Water used in Irrigation on the Physical Properties of Olive Oil in Palestine*, J Material Sci Eng 5:209, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4172/2169-0022.1000209), Table 1, p.2, reports sample S1a (rain-fed, 2013 crop) at 20 °C: **0.9161 g/cm³**. The method identifies a pycnometer. One cm³ equals one mL; no numerical conversion is needed. No uncertainty is reported; none is inferred from printed digits. This is one sample, not a population interval or specification for every olive oil.

Only this attributed scalar is promoted; the paper remains a research reference on `/mnt/storage`. Attribution travels in the registry source and NOTICE.

`olive_oil` is a conserved unresolved mixture. The density is a reference geometry parameter at 293.15 K, held constant elsewhere as an estimate. Composition, heat capacity, thermal expansion, combustion, partition coefficients, emulsions and enzyme kinetics are not supplied. Immiscibility and illustrative display colour are explicit teaching assumptions, not measurements from this paper. The source's irrigation comparisons are not adopted as causal claims.
