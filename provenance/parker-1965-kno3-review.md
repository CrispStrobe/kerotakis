# Potassium nitrate dissolution heat

V. B. Parker, Thermal Properties of Aqueous Uni-univalent Electrolytes
(National Bureau of Standards, 1965), NSRDS-NBS 2, DOI
https://doi.org/10.6028/NBS.NSRDS.2, printed p.30 and table XX.
Republished courtesy of the National Institute of Standards and Technology.

Only the evaluated crystalline KNO3 dissolution enthalpy is transcribed:
8340 +/-30 cal/mol at infinite dilution and 25 C, giving
34.89456 +/-0.12552 kJ/mol using 4.184 J/cal. Positive means cooling.
No finite-concentration or temperature correction is asserted.
