# Crystallisation heat on cooling: coverage (2026-10-27)

Lane: when an aqueous solution is cooled, own solid-phase saturation with its heat in the same energy ledger as the cooling, with an explicit domain and typed refusals outside it. Solvent freezing is out of scope; brine and ice remain with `solve::StateEquilibrator` (PRs #852/#857). Motivating case: TH-007 (50 g water + 30 g KNO3 at 90 C, then `cool v1 12kJ`). On draft #867 it cools on dissolution to 321.76 K and then falls to 264.5 K with no crystals.

## Primary solubility source: the routed database's own phase

`kerotakis_phreeqc::crystallisation_route` classifies every registry solid on every routed database (wateq4f, minteq.v4, pitzer):

- `crystallises-with-priced-heat`: all three of the following hold.
  - The route defines a phase for the solid (its own stablest polymorph, via `derived::routed_phase_for_solid`).
  - The phase states a dissolution enthalpy or an analytic law, so its log K moves with temperature.
  - `enthalpy::species_enthalpy(solid, Solid, route)` prices the solid.

  In that case PHREEQC computes saturation at the vessel temperature in the same solve that speciates the solution, mixed electrolytes and common ions included. The aqueous step's enthalpy balance charges the crystallisation heat.
- `solubility-only`: the phase exists, but it has no temperature law or the solid is unpriced.
- `refused`: the route defines no phase for the solid.

Domain: liquid water, 1 bar, 0-100 C. Above 1 molal the Debye-Hueckel routes keep the router's existing activity-model caveat; pitzer is the brine route. Pricing grows with #867's `solid_route`; the table is recomputed from whatever pricing the build has.

## KNO3 / TH-007

No routed database defines Niter. llnl's Niter under WATEQ activities fails validation, and no nitrate Pitzer parameters are vendored. So KNO3 is `refused` on every route.

At runtime a cooling dose that saturates a K+/NO3- solution now emits the localized refusal `not-modeled.crystallisation-no-routed-phase`. Before, the solution cooled heat-free with no message.

The coupled energy solve in `kerotakis_core::crystallisation` is the mechanism a non-PHREEQC salt would use. It solves `Q = H_sensible + n(T) * dH_diss` against a curve. It is exercised against the Berkeley curve in tests: 12 kJ from 321.76 K gives about 291.4 K and 0.147 mol of crystals, with the energy ledger closed. It runs at runtime only for a curve with `runtime_admitted = true`, and none is.

Other refusals, all localized in EN/DE/FR:

- `crystallisation-heat-unpriced`
- `crystallisation-below-curve-domain`: toward the salt-ice eutectic.
- `crystallisation-not-binary`: applies only to that curve path; where PHREEQC owns the phase, mixed solutions are handled by PHREEQC.

## Remaining gaps

- KNO3, NaNO3 and the other nitrates need a reviewed concentrated-nitrate activity model before any phase is admitted.
- The coupling of PHREEQC-phase crystallisation heat with the `cool` dose needs hosted confirmation on a halite case: the aqueous step's enthalpy balance versus a single-pass `cool`.
- Evaporation-driven crystallisation is not enumerated separately; it goes through the same PHREEQC phases.
- The salt-ice eutectic and freezing stay out of scope.
