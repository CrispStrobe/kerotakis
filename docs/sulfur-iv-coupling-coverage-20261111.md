## Limitations

- Both oxidations are treated as instantaneous on the bench clock; the peroxide path only when the S(IV) pseudo-first-order lifetime is <= MAX_INSTANT_S (a modelling bound, not a constant).
- The I2/I3- split used for the iodine constant is the split at the start of the coupling.
- Rates are written on HSO3-; the shared alpha1 fraction cancels in the competition. A direct SO2(aq) + I2 path and SO3^2- at higher pH are not separately priced.
- Yiin-Margerum constants are 25 C values (no temperature dependence applied); k2/k-1 is held at its 15 C value; k1 is moved with Eyring and dH1.
- Thiosulfate's iodine titration (curated row) runs before the S(IV) coupling inside one tail pass; their competition for iodine within a step is not resolved.
- SO2 booked as gas by the thiosulfate-acid law and still held by the vessel counts as S(IV) while the vessel holds liquid water; vented SO2 is gone.
- Released protons stay in the charge balance (halogen_redox convention); no reaction heat.

Refusals are typed EN/DE/FR (`crates/kerotakis-core/i18n/{de,fr}.toml`, `[not-modeled]`).
The same change also emits a `reacted` event for every kinetic extent below
`OBSERVABLE_MOLES` that is visible in the ledger (`clock::ledger_visible_extent`, one part per
million), so peroxide or oxygen amounts never change without a named reaction.
