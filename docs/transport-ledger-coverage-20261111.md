# Transport ledger coverage (2026-11-11)

Generated by `tools/transport_ledger_coverage.py` (`--check` in CI preflight).

A `transport` inlet declared `reservoir` is a **constant-composition reservoir**: it is not debited,
following the boundary-solution convention of PHREEQC's TRANSPORT keyword (Parkhurst, D.L.,
and Appelo, C.A.J., 2013, *Description of input and examples for PHREEQC version 3*, U.S.
Geological Survey Techniques and Methods, book 6, chap. A43, doi:10.3133/tm6A43). What it
supplied is the source term `inflow_moles` on the `transported` event, which now also names
the `inlet`; every rendered transport line (all registers, EN/DE/FR) states it per species.
Without `reservoir` (the script default since 2026-11-11, or with `drain`) the inlet is a
finite feed whose loss equals the stated inflow; see
docs/transport-source-withdrawal-coverage-20261111.md.

Closure for a reservoir run: **sum over every vessel of (contents + adsorbed surface pool)
- sum of `inflow_moles` over the commands == the initial sum**. No physical constant is
introduced by this lane.

## Not closed by the transport source term

| term | status |
|---|---|
| open-headspace gas exchange during transport (e.g. CO2 with air) | a second open boundary that `inflow_moles` does not record; carbon and H/O are therefore not asserted in the native closure tests |
| free H+/OH- and solute-charge carried by the parcel | moved with the parcel but not listed in `inflow_moles` (not species amounts) |
| PHREEQC surface-complexation / exchange beds in a transport cell | counted by `ConservedLedger`; no dedicated transport closure test yet beyond the native trigger scripts above |
