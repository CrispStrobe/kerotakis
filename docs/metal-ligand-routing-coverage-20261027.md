A complex is any `SOLUTION_SPECIES` reaction whose left-hand master elements are exactly one metal and one ligand (protons and hydroxide aside); ternary species are not counted. Route-deciding families pair a d-/p-block metal with an ammine, thiosulfate, cyanide, thiocyanate, oxalate, citrate, EDTA or acetate ligand. The selector picks the first of wateq4f, minteq.v4 that defines every present family and holds every element; it refuses when a present family is defined nowhere (reviewed known gaps below) or on no single route.
