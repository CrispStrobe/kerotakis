# Iodine chemistry coverage (2026-11-10)

Generated by `tools/iodine_chemistry_coverage.py` from
`crates/kerotakis-core/src/iodine_chemistry.rs`; `iodine_chemistry_coverage_is_current`
fails when the runtime constants drift from the JSON.

The ledger keeps dissolved iodine as one analytical pool `I2(aq)` and iodide as `I-`.
Triiodide is a computed split of those pools at K, used for solubility, optics and
the ferric/iodide equilibrium, so every existing row that reads `I2`/`I-` is unchanged.
Activities are taken as molarities (no activity coefficients); constants are 25 °C.

## Constants

- K(I2 + I- = I3-) = 698 L/mol
- E°(I2(aq)/I-) = 0.621 V; E°(Fe3+/Fe2+) = 0.7696 V; log K(2 Fe3+ + 2 I- = 2 Fe2+ + I2) = 5.0237
- I2 band (centre nm, ε, σ nm) = (460.0, 746.0, 42.0)
- I3- band (centre nm, ε, σ nm) = (353.0, 26400.0, 41.66); fitted ε(460 nm) = 975.3 (cited 975)
- spectrophotometer-parameterised keys: I2

## Mechanisms

| mechanism | how | status |
|---|---|---|
| iodide salts dissolve to ions | existing aqueous route (KI -> K+ + I-); an undissociated Na/K iodide counts as iodide in every iodine computation (halogen_redox::registry_forms) | modelled |
| I2 + I- <=> I3- | computed split of the analytical I2(aq)/I- pools at K; not a separate inventory entry | modelled |
| iodine solubility in iodide | capacity S0 (1 + K I / (1 + K S0)); no supersaturation flag below it; chloride gives no rise | modelled |
| I2 / I3- absorbance | Beer-Lambert over the split with cited epsilon; I3- band width fitted to the two cited points | modelled |
| starch-iodine complex absorbance | eye colour stays the editorial band of starch_iodine.rs; spectrophotometer refuses with not-modeled.starch-iodine-absorptivity (EN/DE/FR) | typed refusal |
| 2 Fe3+ + 2 I- <=> 2 Fe2+ + I2 | solved to log K = 2 (E_Fe - E_I2aq) / 0.05916 with free I2 and I- from the triiodide split, both directions, 0-60 C, ideal activities | modelled |
| I2 + 2 S2O3^2- -> 2 I- + S4O6^2- | existing curated limiting-reagent row, pinned by a closure test | modelled |
| AgI / AgBr solids | registry solids lane (separate branch) | out of scope here |

## Known limits

- No higher polyiodides (I5-) or iodine hydrolysis (HOI, IO-); dissolved iodine above the
  triiodide capacity in very concentrated iodide is under-predicted.
- Fe3+ hydrolysis and chloro-complexation are not subtracted from the analytical Fe3+
  in the ferric/iodide quotient, which over-states the extent in non-acidified water.
- No reaction heats are charged for the iodine rows.
- The starch-iodine complex has no reviewed molar absorptivity: the instrument refuses.
