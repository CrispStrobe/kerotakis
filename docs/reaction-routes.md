# Reaction route families

A route family is one piece of code that applies a whole class of reactions.
Substances and pairs are data rows in its tables, so a new species normally
needs a row (and the species in the registry), not new code. Every family
states what it does not claim through a typed `NotYetModeled` note in EN/DE/FR.

## 1. Potential-driven aqueous redox — `crates/kerotakis-core/src/potential_redox.rs`

An oxidant couple and a reductant couple (reduction halves
`ox + n e⁻ + p H⁺ → r red + w H₂O`, CRC Electrochemical Series potentials)
react until their Nernst potentials meet, using current molalities as
activities (pure solids and liquids and gases at 1 atm have unit activity), or
until a reagent runs out. A residue below `RESOLUTION` (1e-9 of the limiting
reagent) is booked as complete. Protons come from the represented acidity.

| Table | One row is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `COUPLES` | a reduction half: members, registry forms with spectator ions, literal `HX` acids, E° | `MnO4-/Mn+2`, forms `KMnO4` (K⁺) |
| `ROUTES` | an admitted (oxidant couple, reductant couple) pair, with the source that says it is the bench reaction | (`MnO4-/Mn+2`, `I2/I-`) |

Current rows: halogen preparation (MnO₂, KMnO₄, K₂Cr₂O₇ + Cl⁻/Br⁻/I⁻ in acid),
halogen displacement (Cl₂/Br⁻, Cl₂/I⁻, Br₂/I⁻), and iron(II) oxidation (Cl₂,
Br₂, Cr₂O₇²⁻). Chlorine-making and chlorine-oxidant rows run inside
`chlorine.rs` at their old place in its rule order; the rest run in the curated
pass right after it.

Thermodynamics alone never admits a pair: a route needs a source saying the
reaction is fast on the bench (chlorate and iodate are strong and slow).
Iron(II) + permanganate is not a row because the aqueous engine already
equilibrates Mn/Fe redox.

**To add a substance:** add its couple to `COUPLES` (E° with source, half
reaction, registry forms). **To add a pair:** add a `ROUTES` row with its
source. A route whose product is not in the registry yet (Br₂, Cr³⁺) is
dormant: the honesty pass says `not-modeled.potential-redox-product-unregistered`
naming the missing species, and the route starts working when the species is
registered. The unit test `every_route_closes_atoms_and_charge` checks each new
row.

## 2. Concentrated oxidising and dehydrating acids — `crates/kerotakis-core/src/concentrated_sulfuric.rs`

"Concentrated" means the bench's admission boundary: water below half of the
competing liquids (`nonaqueous::AQUEOUS_WATER_FRACTION_FLOOR`).

| Mechanism | Rule | Data |
|---|---|---|
| Dehydration | every registry species whose formula is Cn(H₂O)m, n ≥ 5, m ∈ {n−1, n} → n C + m H₂O, until product water dilutes the acid across the boundary | `DEHYDRATING_ACIDS` |
| Volatile-acid displacement | non-volatile acid + a solid salt `M_aX_b` of a volatile acid `HX` → the acid's hydrogen salt (or neutral salt) + `HX`; gas above its boiling point | `NONVOLATILE_ACIDS`, `VOLATILE_ACIDS` (anion, bp, oxidised-by-acid flag) |
| Oxidation by the acid's couple | acid half balanced against a reductant half by electrons; the acid supplies the protons and the metal's counter-anion | `OXIDISING_ACIDS` (half, E°, medium, admitted reductants), `ACID_REDUCTANTS` |

Salts and products are found in the registry by formula: a new chloride,
acetate or nitrate salt needs no new row. Metal potentials come from the
activity series in `displacement::SERIES`.

Hot concentrated sulfuric acid is far from ideal, so its oxidations are
admitted by the medium (concentrated and at least `HOT_ONSET_K`), not by a
Nernst crossing. Aqueous rows (dilute nitric acid) are admitted by the
standard potentials.

**To add a volatile acid:** add a `VOLATILE_ACIDS` row (acid, anion, CRC
boiling point, whether the acid oxidises it). **To add an oxidising acid or a
reduction product:** add an `OXIDISING_ACIDS` row. **To add a reductant:** add an
`ACID_REDUCTANTS` row and list it under the acids that are reviewed to oxidise
it. Rows whose acid or product is not in the registry are dormant and say
`not-modeled.acid-route-product-unregistered`.

## 3. Generic halogen/oxyhalogen writer — `crates/kerotakis-core/src/halogen_redox.rs`

These are complete-reaction writers built from LLNL-derived halves for pairs
with ΔE° ≥ 0.30 V and a reviewed fast rate (`FAST`). Their coverage table is
generated by `tools/halogen_redox_coverage.py`. Its honesty tail
(`unpriced_notes`) names every present oxidant/reductant pair that no family
prices, and it carries the dormant-row notes of families 1 and 2.

## 4. Metal displacement and acids — `crates/kerotakis-core/src/displacement.rs`

This is the metal activity series (`SERIES`, CRC E° with NBS ΔfH°): metal +
dissolved cation, and metal + acid → H₂.

## Named editorial constants (one per mechanism)

| Constant | Value | Why not a datum |
|---|---|---|
| `concentrated_sulfuric::HOT_ONSET_K` | 373.15 K | Hot conc. H₂SO₄ oxidations are rate-limited, not switched at a temperature, and no reviewed onset exists. |
| `chlorine::DRY_CHLORINATION_K` | 573.15 K | No reviewed ignition temperature of metals in chlorine. |
| `nonaqueous::AQUEOUS_WATER_FRACTION_FLOOR` | 0.5 | The bench's aqueous admission boundary. |

Each constant is stated in the typed note of the rule that uses it.

## Known gaps

- Nitric acid: the `HNO3` rows (NO₃⁻/NO 0.957 V dilute, NO₃⁻/NO₂ 0.803 V
  concentrated, CRC) are written and balanced, but they stay dormant until the
  registry has `HNO3`, `NO`, `NO2` and `Cu(NO3)2`. The concentrated row also
  needs a sourced concentration boundary: commercial 68 % nitric acid is
  water-majority by mole fraction.
- Bromine (`Br2`) and chromium(III) (`Cr+3`) are not in the registry, so the
  bromine, dichromate and Br₂/I⁻ routes are dormant.
- Potassium hydrogen sulfate and potassium sulfate are not in the registry,
  so KCl/KBr/KNO₃ + conc. H₂SO₄ are dormant.
- HBr from a bromide is booked as HBr only. Its partial oxidation by the acid
  to Br₂ + SO₂ has no reviewed split, and a typed note says so.
- Neutral permanganate (MnO₄⁻ → MnO₂) has no couple row yet, and the route
  needs represented acid.
