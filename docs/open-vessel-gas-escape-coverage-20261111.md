# Open-vessel gas escape coverage (2026-11-11)

Generated by `tools/open_vessel_gas_escape_coverage.py --check` from `gas_escape::ESCAPE_TABLE`.
An open vessel releases only gas above its total pressure within a step (fizz); the rest seeps
out on a `wait` at `K_L*A/V` (A = vessel mouth), `1/K_L = 1/k_L + 1/(H k_G)` taken in its
liquid-film limit, `k_L = k_L,ref (D/D_CO2)^(2/3)`. Gases outside the cited liquid-film class or
without data are refused with `not-modeled.gas-escape-no-data` (EN/DE/FR) on a `wait`.
Gases more soluble than CO2 (gas film matters, k_G unsourced) get status `upper_bound`: the
series gas film can only slow transfer, so `k_L*A*(c - c_eq)` bounds the flux from above. It is
reported with `not-modeled.gas-escape-upper-bound-only` (EN/DE/FR) and moves no matter.
Today only CO2 is routed through the aqueous solver (`pending_co2_transfer_mol`); the other
covered rows are priced by the law but no solver yet moves them.

- Diffusivities in water, 25 C: E. L. Cussler, Diffusion: Mass Transfer in Fluid Systems, 2nd ed., Cambridge University Press 1997, ISBN 0-521-45078-0; values as quoted by Wikipedia 'Mass diffusivity' (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_diffusivity, liquid table), read 2026-11-11.
- Henry constants: R. Sander (2015), Atmos. Chem. Phys. 15, 4399-4981, doi:10.5194/acp-15-4399-2015 (table already in properties.rs).
- Liquid-film admission: P. S. Liss (1973), Deep-Sea Res. 20, 221-238, doi:10.1016/0011-7471(73)90013-2 (abstract: O2 and CO2 exchange liquid-phase controlled).
- NOT sourced: a still-air gas-side velocity k_G (Hicks & Liss 1976, Tellus 28, 348-354, not readable). Gases more soluble than CO2 therefore get only a liquid-film upper bound.
- NOT used: the 1.4e-6 m/s 'smooth-surface' figure attributed to Liss & Merlivat (1986); their smooth regime is 0.17*u10 cm/h, which is zero at no wind, so 1.4e-6 corresponds to u10 ~ 3 m/s and is not a still-air value.
